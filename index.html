Bulk Bills

A Chrome extension to bulk-edit draft bills (works with Xero): account codes, tracking, line descriptions, due dates, references and suppliers. Every change is previewed first and can be undone for 7 days.

Add to Chrome How to use Bulk Bills

What it does

Xero can bulk-approve draft bills, but changing the account, tracking, due date, reference or supplier still means opening each bill. Find and recode is for bills that are already posted, so it does not help the drafts in your queue.

Bulk Bills lets you fix lots of draft bills at once. You can combine several kinds of change in one batch:

  • Account code: on every line, or only lines currently on a given account
  • Tracking: set or clear up to 2 tracking categories (optionally only on lines with a given account)
  • Line description: find and replace, add a prefix or suffix
  • Due date: set a date, or bill date plus a number of days
  • Reference: set, prefix, suffix, or find and replace
  • Supplier (contact): change to another active contact

Before anything is written, you get a preview of every bill and line (old to new), with subtotal, VAT and total before and after. If a change would alter an amount, tax rate, quantity or currency, that bill is blocked. You can then apply the batch and, if you want, add a History note to each bill.

Who it is for

Bookkeepers, small practices and small businesses with piles of draft bills to code — for example bills that arrived by email or from a receipt app with the wrong account or no tracking.

How it differs

It only works on draft bills. Approved, paid and voided bills are never touched. Each bill is re-checked just before it is written, and anything that has changed in the meantime is skipped. After writing, each bill is re-read to confirm that amounts and VAT have not changed. Every batch can be undone for 7 days, and you can download a CSV change report.

Trial and price

  • Free trial: 10 bill changes, once. No card needed.
  • Pro: £12/month or £120/year, up to 3 organisations. Payments are taken securely by Stripe. Cancel any time.

If you don’t pick a plan, Bulk Bills disconnects from that organisation after 14 days. Your bills in Xero are not affected.

Your data

Your bills and accounting data go only between your browser and Xero’s official API. They are never sent to us. Our licence server keeps only what it needs to run your trial or plan (your Xero user ID and email, connected organisation IDs, and a count of bill changes). No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold. Read the privacy policy.
