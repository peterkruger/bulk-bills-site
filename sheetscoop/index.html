SheetScoop

A Chrome extension that turns lists, product grids, search results and tables on any web page into a clean spreadsheet: CSV, Excel or Google Sheets. It can follow the Next button, numbered pages, Load more and infinite scroll, so one click covers several pages.

Add to Chrome Ask a question

How it works

  1. Open a page with a list, grid or table.
  2. Click the SheetScoop icon in the toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+S). It finds the repeating data on the page.
  3. Pick the dataset, untick or rename columns, then press Scrape this page or Scrape pages.
  4. Export to CSV or Excel, copy it, or open it in Google Sheets.

It reads the page the way you see it in your own tab, waits between pages and skips duplicate rows.

Free and Pro

  • Free: 10 scrapes a month, each up to 5 pages. No row limits and no sign-up. CSV, Excel, copy and Open in Google Sheets.
  • Pro trial: 3 days, once, no card needed: up to 20 scrapes of 25 pages and 5 AI tidy-ups.
  • Pro: £6/month or £49/year, flat. Unlimited scrapes and pages, direct export to Google Sheets and AI column tidy-ups (clear column names, empty and duplicate columns removed). No credits. Payments are taken securely by Stripe. Cancel any time from the extension’s options page (Manage subscription); Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Changed computer or reinstalled? Use Restore purchase on the options page: we email a 6-digit code to the address you used at checkout.

Your data

Scraping runs in your own browser tab, and scraped rows stay on your computer unless you choose to send them somewhere. Send to Google Sheets writes straight from your browser to your own Google account. AI tidy columns sends the column names and up to 20 sample rows to our server, which asks OpenAI for suggestions and keeps nothing after the reply. No analytics, no ads, and your data is never sold.
