How to use Bulk Bills

Bulk Bills is a Chrome extension that bulk-edits draft bills (works with Xero). This page covers installing it, making a change, undoing one, and what the trial includes.

Add to Chrome Back to Bulk Bills

1. Install

  1. Open the Bulk Bills listing in the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then confirm.
  3. Open the Bulk Bills panel from the extension. You can also turn on an optional button on the Xero bills page (go.xero.com) that opens the same panel.

2. Connect to Xero

In the panel, connect the Xero organisation you want to work on. You sign in with Xero’s own sign-in. We never see your password.

You can turn on Stay connected if you want to stay signed in after you close Chrome. To remove access later, use Disconnect this organisation in the panel, or go to Settings → Connected apps in Xero.

3. Select draft bills

Bulk Bills only works with draft bills. Approved, paid and voided bills are never shown for editing and are never touched.

Filter the drafts by supplier, bill date, due date, reference, account code or tracking, then select the bills you want to change.

4. Choose the changes

Pick one change, or combine several in the same batch:

  • Account code: on every line, or only lines currently on a given account
  • Tracking: set or clear up to 2 tracking categories (optionally only on lines with a given account)
  • Line description: find and replace, add a prefix or suffix
  • Due date: set a date, or bill date plus a number of days
  • Reference: set, prefix, suffix, or find and replace
  • Supplier (contact): change to another active contact

5. Preview

Review the preview before anything is written. It shows every bill and line, old to new, with subtotal, VAT and total before and after.

If a change would alter an amount, tax rate, quantity or currency, that bill is blocked and is not included in the write.

6. Apply

Apply the changes in a batch. You get a pass or fail result for each bill. Optionally tick Add a History note to write a short note describing the change to each bill’s history in Xero.

Just before a bill is written, it is re-checked. If it has changed in the meantime, it is skipped. After writing, each bill is re-read to confirm that amounts and VAT have not changed.

7. Undo within 7 days

Every batch can be undone for 7 days. You can also download a CSV change report for a batch.

Batch history (the data used for undo and the change report) is kept on your computer and is deleted automatically after 7 days, when you click Forget or Hide results, or when you remove the extension.

Trial and upgrade

  • Free trial: 10 bill changes, once. No card needed.
  • Pro: £12/month or £120/year, up to 3 organisations. Payments are taken securely by Stripe on Stripe’s own checkout page. Cancel any time (use the link in your Stripe receipt email, or email support).

If you don’t pick a plan, Bulk Bills disconnects from that organisation after 14 days. Your bills in Xero are not affected. Reminder emails may be sent on day 7 and day 12, and a notice on day 14; each email has an unsubscribe link, and they stop if you pay.

Troubleshooting

The panel does not connect to Xero

Use Xero’s sign-in in the panel and approve access for the organisation you want. If it is already listed under Settings → Connected apps in Xero, you can disconnect it there and connect again from Bulk Bills.

I can’t find a bill

Only draft bills appear. Check your filters (supplier, dates, reference, account code or tracking), and that you have connected the right organisation.

A bill was blocked or skipped

A bill is blocked if the change would alter an amount, tax rate, quantity or currency. A bill is skipped if it changed in Xero after you previewed it and before it was written. Check the pass/fail result for that bill, or download the CSV change report.

I can’t undo a batch

Undo is available for 7 days. After that, or if you clicked Forget or Hide results, or removed the extension, the batch history is no longer there. You would need to edit those drafts in Xero yourself.

Bulk Bills disconnected after 14 days

If no plan was picked, Bulk Bills disconnects from that organisation after 14 days. Your bills in Xero are unchanged. Connect again and pick a plan if you want to keep using it.

Payments or cancelling

Checkout is handled by Stripe. We never see your card details. To cancel a paid plan, use the link in your Stripe receipt email or email us.
